The big picture

A bill comes in, you approve it, then you invoice the customer

Most of the work happens on its own. You only step in twice: once to approve the supplier's bill, and once to send the customer's invoice.

Before the portal, every bill and invoice was handled by hand, across several applications. You found the bill in Outlook, opened the PDF, looked up the order in the DBM Supplies portal, checked each price against the price list, typed the bill into Xero, marked the purchase order billed, then built the customer's invoice and emailed it. Each step meant switching to another screen, and each switch was a chance to miss something.

Now the portal does the reading, matching, checking and typing for you. You see the supplier's PDF, our order, the price check and the Xero result together, in one place, and you only make the decisions: approve, fix or query.

Before · by hand
  1. Outlook: find the supplier's email and open the PDF
  2. DBM Supplies portal: look up the order and what was sold
  3. Price list: check every line's price and quantity
  4. Xero: type in the bill and mark the order billed
  5. Xero and Outlook: build the customer's invoice and email it
  6. Outlook: file the email away by hand
Now · in the Accounts Portal
  • Bills are read from the mailbox by themselves, every 5 minutes
  • Every line is checked against our order and price list for you
  • One click on Approve puts the bill in Xero and marks the order billed
  • One click on Approve & send invoices and emails the customer
  • Emails are filed into folders for you

No inbox to keep tidy. Once a bill is read, the portal files its email into the right folder of the invoices@dbmsupplies.com.au mailbox: under the supplier's name, under DBM, or under Other for anything that isn't a bill. Only emails that need a person stay in the Inbox.

User role access

Who can do what

Every person has a level in each portal: Off, Staff or Admin. DBM Supplies' AP and AR are separate portals from DBM's, so a person can be Staff in DBM Supplies AP, Admin in DBM AP, and Off in DBM Supplies AR, all at once.

Off

The portal doesn't show for them. DBM Supplies only appears in the company switch for people with access to it.

Staff

Does the daily work: checks, approves, rejects, fixes and sends.

Admin

Everything Staff does, plus handing out and tidying up the work.

Super admin

Admin in every portal of every company, and the only role that manages Users.

Step 2 · happens on its own

Where the bills come from

Suppliers email DBM Supplies' bills to payables@dbmsupplies.com.au. That mailbox passes every email straight on to invoices@dbmsupplies.com.au. Every 5 minutes the portal opens invoices@, turns each bill into a bill in AP, and files the email away.

You don't need to forward or type anything in. If a supplier says they've sent a bill, give it 5 minutes and look in AP.

StyrofoamWaffle Pods

Read straight off the bill's PDF. Free lines count too: a $0 scrap bag still counts toward the order.

ARCReinforcement

Read straight off the bill's PDF.

DBMConcrete, Rediwall, Reinforcement

Built from DBM's own sales order to DBM Supplies, with the emailed PDF attached.

Anything elseStripe notices, receipts

Not a bill, so nothing is made. The email is filed under Other.

If the portal can't read an ARC or Styrofoam email (for example, there's no invoice number, or it's billed to DBM instead of DBM Supplies), it leaves the email in the mailbox's Inbox for a person to look at.

Good to know about ARC. ARC uses its own item codes. The portal translates them to ours, so don't worry if the codes on the PDF look different.

ARC rounds weights on each line, so a weight that's within 1% of the order counts as a match. Things you count, like trench mesh and freight, must match exactly.

Each line also shows the Xero account it will be posted to, and the bill shows the customer the order is for.

Step 5

Approving a bill

Approving sends the bill to DBM Supplies' own Xero (never DBM's), with its PDF attached.

For now, approving needs DBM's AP access as well as DBM Supplies'. If Approve says "You don't have access to AP", ask an admin. See To be added.

1

Check the lines against the PDF

Look down the two label columns. Anything red needs a decision.

2

Fix anything that's wrong

Click the pencil on a line to change its quantity, price, item code or Xero account.

3

Click Approve

You'll find it in the Review & approve box. If the bill is marked Review Required, type a short note saying why it's OK, for example "ARC confirmed the extra 20 bars".

Not right? Click Reject and give a reason.

Credit notes

Credit notes arrive on their own, but go into Xero by hand for now

When DBM gives DBM Supplies a credit (for example CN-0556), the portal brings it in from DBM's Xero by itself. It shows in AP as Review Required, and its reference names the invoice it's crediting.

  • Check the amount, and the invoice it credits, against the PDF.
  • A credit from Styrofoam or ARC that arrives by email is read the same way as a bill.

The portal can't send credit notes to Xero yet. Enter them in DBM Supplies' Xero yourself.

Step 6 · AR

Invoicing the customer

An order shows up in AR only after its supplier bill has been approved in AP and is in Xero. Approve the bill first, and the order follows.

Where the lines come from

The customer, items, quantities and prices come from the order in the DBM Supplies portal.

The invoice already exists

The DBM Supplies portal has already started the invoice in Xero, numbered like the order (DS1002081). AR finishes that one. It never makes a second invoice.

The portal compares what the supplier billed with what we sold. It holds an order as Review Required when:

  • the supplier billed more or less of an item than we sold,
  • we're selling below cost,
  • the order isn't in the DBM Supplies portal, or
  • the customer isn't set up as a contact in Xero.
1

Open the order and check its lines

Use the pencil on a line to fix anything that's wrong.

2

Click Approve & send

The portal puts the lines on the invoice in Xero, approves it, and emails it to the customer with the PDF attached, from ar@dbmsupplies.com.au. A copy of every invoice sent is kept in that mailbox's Sent Items.

Want to hold the email back? Untick Email it to the customer now. Send it later with Send to customer on the order.

Three things to know first

  1. Access. To approve, assign, archive or change a DBM Supplies bill, you currently also need DBM's AP access. With DBM Supplies access alone you can look but not save. The same goes for admin-only buttons in AP and AR (Assign work, Archived): they show only to DBM admins.

  3. Don't tick "Save to price list" or use "Add to price list" on a DBM Supplies line. They write to DBM's price lists, not DBM Supplies'.
