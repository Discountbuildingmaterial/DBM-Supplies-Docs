The big picture

Your calls, texts and emails for the day, ready each morning

Collections reads DBM Supplies' Xero and works out who to chase today: who to call, who only needs a reminder, and whose promise to pay falls due. You make the calls and press send. Every step is written down, so anyone can pick up where you left off.

Nothing goes by itself

Every call, text and email goes only when you press it and say yes. Automatic sending is switched off.

A fresh list every morning

Today fills itself at 7:35 each morning, the most pressing customers first. A step closes by itself once the customer pays.

From DBM Supplies' Xero

Invoices, payments and contact details are read from DBM Supplies' Xero every 15 minutes. Nothing is changed in Xero.

Who's next

One row per customer, most pressing first: calls left from an earlier day, then broken promises, then customers over their limit, then the biggest amounts. The small dots show what's left for them; a tick means done.

Do this next

Call and Text open DBM Call with their number ready, from your own Dialpad line. Send email sends from DBM Supplies' mailbox with their pay link. Each one asks you first.

Mark sent and Skip

Mark sent is for a message you sent another way. Skip is for one that isn't needed. Both ask first.

Reminders only

Customers who only need an email or a text today. Send all sends the reminder emails one after another: it asks first, and Stop halts it. Texts go one at a time from DBM Call.

Promises due

Promises falling due today, or missed yesterday. A missed promise comes back as a call.

Done

What's been done in the last two weeks, and who did it. Made a mistake? Open it and press Reopen.

Missing details

Customers who owe money but have no phone or email in Xero. Add them in DBM Supplies' Xero and they drop off within 15 minutes.

Step 4

After a call: say how it went

Press Say how the call went and pick one. What you pick decides when the customer comes back on the list.

How it wentWhat happens next
Promised to payGive the date they'll pay by, and the amount if they said one. They're not called before then. Still overdue the day after, and they're back near the top as a broken promise.
Says it's paidToday's step is done. Once the payment reaches Xero, their steps close by themselves.
DisputedAdd a note saying what's in dispute, so the next person knows. It shows on their file.
No answer Left a messageThey're called again a week on, while they're still overdue.
OtherAdd a note to say how the call went.
Follow up on (optional). With any outcome, pick a date to come back to them. They won't be called before it, and they're back on Today that morning while they still owe anything. With a promise too, whichever date comes first.
Spoke to them? Today's email and text to that customer can be skipped, so they don't get a reminder straight after your call.
Step 5

Customers and their files

Who needs attention

The tiles at the top: overdue, on hold, over their limit, unapplied credit, and accounts that might be the same business. Press one to list them.

Find anyone

Search by name, ABN or account number, or narrow the list with the filters. Each row shows what's overdue and how old, the limit, their standing, when they last paid and when we last spoke.

Same business?

Accounts with no ABN in Xero whose name reads like another customer's. Say whether they're the same business; it's only asked once.

A customer's file

Press a name to open it: their unpaid invoices, Statement and Email statement, and everything that's happened with them.

Export file

The whole file as one zip: the timeline as a PDF, each call's transcript and recording link, each text conversation, each email and the letters. It asks first.

Legal hold · Admin

Only for a customer whose debt is going legal. Collections stops chasing them, and their DBM Supplies limit goes to $0 so they can't order. It shows in red with who placed it, when and why. Lifting it puts their limit back exactly as it was.

Step 6

Credit limits

Pick Credit limits in the left bar. For DBM Supplies it shows each customer's Approved Limit from the DBM Supplies portal's Customer credit, and what they owe from Xero.

Change a limit

Anyone can lower a limit. Setting a first limit or raising one needs an Admin. Both ask first, and the change is saved straight to the DBM Supplies portal.

What stops ordering

An account customer with no credit left can't order in the DBM Supplies portal. A cash (COD) customer is only stopped once they owe $25,000.

No separate hold button

DBM Supplies has no on/off hold. To stop a customer ordering while their debt goes legal, an Admin uses Legal hold on their file.

Insurance

Record a customer's credit insurance on their row, so everyone can see what's covered.

NIL isn't a hold. A limit of NIL in the DBM Supplies portal turns the credit check off, so the customer can order without limit. Use Legal hold, not NIL, to stop someone.
Step 7

The other pages

Payments

What customers paid by card on their pay links, and whether it's in Xero yet. Accounts records each one in Xero by hand; it shows as In Xero within 15 minutes. Waiting three days or more turns red.

Activity

Calls, texts, emails and promises over a period you pick. Press Details on a call for DBM Call's summary and transcript once they're ready.

Scoreboard

The numbers: what's overdue, what's been collected this month, how long customers take to pay, and what's owed by age.

Reminders

A preview of what the reminder ladder would send, day by day, to whom and in what words. Nothing is sent from here; the ones to send are on Today.

Reports

Download Excel (every customer and unpaid invoice) or Word (a written report of two or three pages). It asks first.

Settings · Admin

Who reminders can reach (nobody, the test list or customers), when each reminder goes as an invoice ages, and its wording, with a preview using a customer's own figures.

Quick answers

Questions people ask

Will it ever text or email a customer by itself?

No. Automatic sending is off. Every call, text and email goes only when you press it and say yes.

I saved the wrong outcome for a call.

Open the Done tile on Today, find it, and press Reopen. Then say how it really went.

How do I get a customer back on my list on a certain day?

When you say how the call went, pick a date in Follow up on. They're back on Today that morning.

A customer paid by card but still shows as owing.

Look in Payments. Once Accounts records it in Xero, it updates within 15 minutes.

A customer has no phone or email.

They're under Missing details on Today. Add them in DBM Supplies' Xero and they come through within 15 minutes.

I can't see Collections, a page, or DBM Supplies.

You only see what you've been given. Ask a super admin to change it under Users on the DBM Supplies home.

Where's the guide inside the portal?

Press Guide at the top of any Collections page for a tour on the real screen, or just the part you need.
